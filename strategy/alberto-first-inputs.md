# What we need from Alberto

We can start with **one machine or equipment type and its specifications**, then build the range continuously. Short notes, photos and existing brochures are enough to begin preparing the content.

1. **Choose the first machine to present.** Give us its type, brand and model, where known. Explain what work it does and which customers it is intended for.

2. **Send its specifications and pictures.** Share the brochure or specification sheet and available photos. Point out the details a buyer should notice first, plus any different versions of this machine. This gives us the starting material for its product page and selection view.

3. **Explain why a customer would choose it.** Tell us its one or two strongest advantages and what they mean in practice. If comparing it with another machine, name that alternative and explain the difference. Also describe what COMQ adds through its advice or service.

4. **Confirm what COMQ can offer.** Is it for sale, rental or both? Is it new or used? Confirm availability, delivery destinations and timing, and what support or maintenance is included. Explain what you need from a customer to prepare a quote.

5. **Prepare LinkedIn; publish after the first machine is live.** Describe who COMQ helps and what it wants to achieve, and confirm who will manage the company page. The profile and draft posts can be prepared in parallel. **Only publish social media posts once the first machine and its specifications are on the website**, so the audience has a concrete product to explore and a useful reason to visit.

6. **Choose the first article topic.** Pick one question customers often ask about the first machine. Give us your explanation, a practical example and the advice you would offer. Notes are enough to draft an article; you can review it before publication.

7. **Keep adding information, then explain how you recommend equipment.** Send the next machines and their details as they become available. For the later guided selection, describe the questions you ask about a mine and how the answers change your choice of machine or version. Customer questions can guide what we add next.

Start with **items 1 and 2**. We can prepare the first product view while developing the remaining content together.
